MilkDrop preset glyph — proposals

Each concept is drawn in the same 16:9 slot as the placeholder: large on top, then at the actual 39 × 22 px size (the row is 22 px tall; the frame kisses its edges). A single trail instance is scaled 1.30× and offset (−8, −2) viewBox units — roughly 3–5 px behind at this size — at 30% intensity. The brief is abstract/geometric — the visuals, not a photo icon. The current placeholder is first; 16 alternatives follow.
